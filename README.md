Slide 2 – Proposed Solution

Let me start with a simple question.

Are we maintaining computers, or are we mainly fixing them when something goes wrong?

Our proposed solution aims to make PC maintenance smarter through automation.

First, we propose automatic PC health monitoring instead of manual checks.

Second, the system can identify early warning signs of hardware problems and alert the maintenance team.

Third, AI can help analyse complaints, identify their categories and suggest priorities.

We also propose automatic maintenance reminders, spare-parts tracking and centralised reports.

The main aim is to find problems early, reduce manual work and avoid unexpected PC downtime.

In simple words, we want to move from fixing problems after they happen to preventing them before they happen.

Slide 3 – Six Connected Modules

To achieve this, we propose the PCM Intelligent Automation Portal, which has six connected modules.

First, PC Asset Management – stores PC details like asset ID, department and location.

Second, AI PC Health Monitoring – monitors CPU, RAM, disk space and storage health.

Third, Smart Complaint Management – uses AI to analyse complaints and suggest their priority.

Fourth, Predictive Maintenance – helps identify possible hardware problems early.

Fifth, Spare Parts Management – tracks available spare parts and gives low-stock alerts.

Finally, Analytics and Reporting – gives an overall view of PC health, complaints and maintenance.

All six modules work together.

For example, if a PC shows a health warning, the portal can create a maintenance task, check spare-parts availability and update the maintenance history after repair.

This portal is designed to support our existing systems, not replace SAP.

Our goal is to make PC maintenance easier, smarter and more efficient.
